# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Egress is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community.
Participants are expected to:

- be respectful of differing experiences and viewpoints;
- give and accept useful, good-faith feedback;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's sensitive data; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and work toward repair.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or in
public interactions representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the maintainer's [GitHub
profile](https://github.com/Yanis897349). Do not use a public issue for a
conduct report. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports
will be reviewed as privately and impartially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially
represents the project elsewhere.
